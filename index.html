Est. 1962

It started in Grandpa's kitchen.

Before the jumbo burgers and the catfish, there was a pie. Grandpa baked sweet potato pies for church suppers, birthdays, and any neighbor who needed one. Folks kept asking. He kept baking.

Today the next generation runs the kitchen on Colonel Glenn and East Broadway. Same recipe. Same patience. Same pride in feeding Little Rock like family.

“Don't rush the sweet potatoes. They'll tell you when they're ready.”

Grandpa's first rule of pie
